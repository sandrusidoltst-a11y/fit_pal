# Cut-Phase Method Rules (Condensed)

Source: `docs/nutrition-method.md`. Only the rules relevant to building a first cut plan are included here.

## Phase classification

| Body fat % | Protocol |
|---|---|
| > 25% | Overweight — protein/carbs calculated on target weight (20% BF), extend fast up to 18h, 2 meals/day allowed |
| 13–25% | Standard cut |
| < 13% | Low-BF — protein 2.3 g/kg over 3 meals, reduce deficit, fast max 12h |

## Formulas

**TDEE (Mifflin-St Jeor × activity)**
- Male BMR = `10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5`
- Female BMR = `10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age − 161`
- Activity: 1.2 sedentary / 1.375 light / 1.55 moderate / 1.725 heavy

**Max deficit**: `70 × kg_of_body_fat` (never exceed — tissue breakdown = muscle loss)
**Target deficit**: `min(25% of TDEE, max_deficit)`
**Cumulative math**: 8,000 cal deficit = 1 kg fat loss (so 500 cal/day ≈ 0.5 kg/week)

## Protein rules

- 2.0 g/kg (cut, recomp, bulk)
- 2.3 g/kg when BF < 13%, spread across 3 meals
- 20g minimum per serving, 2–3 servings/day
- Only complete proteins count: meat, poultry, fish, eggs, dairy, soy/tofu
- Legumes, cereals, seitan (without Lysine) do NOT count
- Post-workout protein must be complete

## Carbohydrate rules

- Minimum by training intensity:
  - 1 session/week: 0.75 g/kg
  - 2 sessions: 1.0 g/kg
  - 3+ sessions: 1.25 g/kg
- During cut, typical total is ~100g/day
- Portions capped at 50g per non-post-workout meal
- Post-workout is the priority carb slot (loading meal)
- Carbs can be transferred between days (weekly average matters)
- Preferred sources: rice, potato, white bread (high-GI starch)
- Avoid during cut: legumes, oats, cereals (low GI, sustained insulin)

## Fat rules

- Do not add fat intentionally — it comes with protein
- Verify the implied fat grams land in ~0.8–1.2 g/kg using mid-range protein sources
- No fat before workout (slow digestion)
- Keep post-workout meals lean

## Meal timing (cut)

- No food for first 3 hours after waking
- Daily fast: 12–14h (up to 18h if BF > 30%; max 12h if BF < 13%)
- Delay carbs to 7+ hours after waking (ideal, not mandatory)
- Fixed meal times every day (reduces ghrelin surges)
- Post-workout meal 1h after session, no later than 2h
- Stop eating ~2h before bed

## Pre-workout

- Ideal: empty stomach + caffeine (3 mg/kg, 30–40 min before)
- If must eat: lean protein only, 40–60 min before
- 1 tsp black coffee ≈ 80–90 mg caffeine

## Post-workout loading meal

- Most important meal of the day for body composition
- 1h after workout, not immediately
- Lean protein + simple starch, no vegetables (fiber slows digestion)
- Can be a "treat meal": pizza, toast, sushi, burger, etc.
- If splitting into two: up to 5h window from end of workout

## Discretionary calories

- 100 cal/day reserved, can accumulate across days
- Example 100-cal units: 3 chaser shots, 60ml whiskey, 125ml wine, 1/3 beer, 1 apple, 1 banana, 3 dates, 450g watermelon, 6 chocolate squares, half Kinder Bueno row, 2 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp raw tahini, 1.5 tbsp olive oil, 250ml juice, 5 tsp sugar, 3 tsp peanut butter

## Free foods (unlimited when hungry)

- Mushrooms, pickled cucumbers, hearts of palm, skinny pasta (9 cal/100g)
- Coffee, tea (no sugar; sweeteners OK)
- Zero drinks during meals only, not between
- Dry spices freely
- Up to 200ml milk/day total

## Forbidden during cut (except in discretionary)

- Fat-heavy foods: avocado, tahini, olives, almonds, nuts, peanut butter
- Low-GI carbs: white beans, peas, corn, bulgur, chickpeas, fava, quinoa, sweet potato, oats

## Weighing rules

- Weigh BEFORE cooking: meat, fish, vegetables, potatoes
- Weigh AFTER cooking: rice, pasta, bread, couscous
- If only possible after cooking, subtract 25% from the plan amount

## Training alignment

- Minimum 2 strength sessions/week (prevents muscle breakdown)
- For building: 3/week
- Max 3/week during cut (recovery harder in deficit)
- No consecutive training days
- Do not skip post-workout loading meal even after a bad week

## Adjustments (not first-plan, but note for later)

- After 2–3 weeks, if fat loss < 1–3%/month (women) or 2–4%/month (men): reduce carbs
- If performance drops + hunger climbs: double-load session then find middle ground
- After losing weight: recompute TDEE (less body = less burn)
- If stalled: check hidden calories (sauces, oil, tahini, alcohol) first

## Red flags to call out in the plan

- Deficit exceeding `70 × kg_fat` → reject, cap at that number
- Protein under 2 g/kg → reject
- Any breakfast within 3 hours of waking → note as violation
- Post-workout skipped → call out as highest-priority fix
