## Rules

### Planning
- Plan your options the day before, not in the moment
- Fixed meal times prevent unexpected hunger
- Prep groceries in advance; pick restaurants that fit the plan

### Fasting & timing
- No food in the first 3 hours after waking
- Daily fast: 12–14 hours
- Stop eating ~2 hours before bed
- Ideal: carbs delayed to 7+ hours after waking

### Pre-workout
- Ideal: empty stomach
- Strong black coffee 30–40 min before (3 mg/kg caffeine)
- If you must eat: lean protein 40–60 min before

### Post-workout
- Eat ~1 hour after (not immediately)
- Split into two meals within 4–5 hours if too much at once
- Never skip, even after a poor week

### Hydration
- 3.7L water/day (men) / 2.7L/day (women) / 40ml per kg
- Pinch of healthy salt in your water bottle
- Extra water on high-carb post-workout days

### Weighing
- Weigh BEFORE cooking: meat, fish, vegetables, potato
- Weigh AFTER cooking: rice, pasta, bread, couscous
- If only possible after cooking (meat): subtract 25%

### Discretionary — 100 kcal/day (can accumulate)

Examples: 3 shots / 60ml whiskey / 125ml wine / 1/3 beer / 1 apple / 1 banana / 3 dates / 450g watermelon / 6 chocolate squares / half Kinder Bueno row / 2 tbsp mayo / 1 tbsp raw tahini / 1.5 tbsp olive oil / 250ml juice / 5 tsp sugar / 3 tsp peanut butter

### Free foods (unlimited when hungry)
- Mushrooms, pickles, hearts of palm, skinny pasta (9 kcal/100g)
- Add extra lean protein between meals if starving
- Coffee/tea without sugar — free

### Forbidden during cut (except within discretionary)
- Fatty foods: avocado, tahini, olives, almonds, nuts, peanut butter
- Low-GI carbs: white beans, peas, corn, bulgur, chickpeas, fava, quinoa, sweet potato, oats

### Alcohol
- Not on training days (impairs glycogen filling and muscle building)
- Count against discretionary
- Spirits + zero mixer preferred over beer/wine

### Early-wake adjustment
- If waking early, swap lunch and mid-day snack positions

### Post-run (>6km)
- Add an extra mid-day style snack (carb + protein combo)

### Post-workout splitting
- Can split into 2 meals within 4 hours of session end

### Do not skip post-workout
- Even after a week of slip-ups — this is the highest priority meal

## Progress tracking

- Weigh at a fixed morning time (post-bathroom, no clothes)
- 2 weigh-ins/week at equal intervals, compare weekly averages
- Ignore the first week's data
- Take tape measurements (arm/chest/waist) every 2 weeks, same position
- If uncomfortable with the scale: measurements only

## Notes

- This is your **first plan** — designed to be safe and livable, not optimal.
- Expect a 1–2 week adjustment period (mild hunger in the first hours is normal).
- Weight swings ±2kg overnight are water/glycogen, not fat (1kg fat = 8,000 kcal surplus).
- Reassess after 2–3 weeks using real data. Adjustments (tighter deficit, source swaps, zero-carb rest days) come later.
